Using the /3d-print-designer skill: a print report on the ball-joint phone mount. Before printing the mount I printed a test coupon of sockets for its 10 mm ball, in PETG on my Bambu X1C (0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls). The sockets had bores of 10.2, 10.4 and 10.6 mm, each split by four 0.8 mm relief slots. 10.2 wouldn't snap on at all. 10.4 snapped on with a firm push and holds the phone at any angle without drooping, even on a bumpy drive. 10.6 drooped under the phone's weight. I've printed the real mount with the 10.4 socket and it's working well. No changes to the model needed.
