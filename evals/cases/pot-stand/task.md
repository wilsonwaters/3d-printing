Use the /3d-print-designer skill to make a stand for a square glazed ceramic plant pot that lives outside on pavers.

- Lift the pot 25 mm off the ground so the drain hole in the centre of its base drains freely, and let water run out sideways.
- One design should suit pots with square bases anywhere from 170 mm to 200 mm.
- A full, wet pot weighs up to 30 kg.

Printer: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (256 mm bed), 0.4 mm nozzle. Material: PETG.
