Use the /3d-print-designer skill to design a snap-fit enclosure for a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B.

- A base and a lid that snap together without screws.
- Openings for the USB-C power, both micro-HDMI ports, the audio jack, both USB pairs and Ethernet; the microSD slot must stay reachable.
- Ventilation over the SoC.
- The board sits on four standoffs using its M2.5 mounting holes.

Printer: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon, 0.4 mm nozzle. Material: PETG.
The .scad and STLs are enough.
